## Requirements {#requirements}

1. **R1 Signals**, all computed read-only on catalog/Lance copies:
   - (a) entity-set **Jaccard**;
   - (b) **lift** and **NPMI** of the two concepts' entity sets, with **support as a gate**
     (`|A ∩ B| ≥ s`), never a multiplier;
   - (c) concept-vector **cosine**, percentile-normalized within its store (absolute cosines are
     meaningless: the memory-compile lesson);
   - (d) **name normalization** (the 20.1 rule) as a binary signal;
   - (e) a **fusion**: (a)/(b) gated by (d) or by (c)'s percentile.
2. **R2 Metrics** per store and pooled:
   - AUC on silver and on gold;
   - precision@k on gold (k = 25, 50, 100);
   - precision on the symbol-vs-word stratum by itself (the trap class).
3. **R3 Recommendation.** Either one rule, "merge when <signal> ≥ θ (and gate)", with θ chosen on
   gold where precision is ≥ 0.95 on the trap stratum, or an explicit "no signal is safe at
   precision 0.95", with the best curve shown.
4. **R4 Shape-aware.** Report each signal's results split by df bucket (hapax, 2–4, 5–20, 21+,
   hubs = top 1%). The adjacency-shape spike shows the degree distribution is heavy-tailed, and a
   signal that only works on mid-df concepts must say so.

## Acceptance criteria {#acceptance}

1. A table of signals × stores × {AUC silver, AUC gold, P@k gold, trap precision} in the
   implementation summary, generated by a committed script that reruns byte-identically on the
   same copies.
2. A recommendation per R3, with its θ and the curve.
3. No write to any store. A test asserts the script opens catalogs `read_only`.
