## What the native mechanisms change, in one paragraph {#summary}

They make **rule delivery** buildable now: denies, path rules, skills by trigger, commit checks,
agent-start context (T1 to T5, T7). They do not touch **what the per-prompt push carries or
whether it was used** (T6), which is what rows 12, 14, 15 and plan-19 are. So the evidence moves
work **forward**, not back: two post-mod items come in, one row-12 task moves up, three pieces
merge into I1, and one cut gains a dependency. Nothing on rows 12, 14 or 15 moves post-mod on
the evidence. If the user wants load taken off the queue to pay for I1's eight tasks, that is a
trade, and [[#offset]] names the cheapest things to defer. {#summary-body}
