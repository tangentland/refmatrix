## Checked and not filed {#not-filed}

- **Env inheritance.** The daemon grandchild inherits `RMX_DAEMON_SPAWN_WAIT=30.0`
  (`ps eww` on the probe daemon). This does no harm. Nothing that runs inside a daemon process
  starts another daemon: no `spawn_daemon*`, `ensure_global_daemon` or bus accept is reachable
  from `daemon.py`'s op paths. The only readers of the variable are `spawn_daemon`'s default and
  this caller, which overrides it. If a later change gives the daemon a way to start other
  daemons, those starts would wait 30 s instead of 15 s. That changes only how long they wait,
  and a start could not be wrongly reported as failed.
- **Cost of the r6 prescription: a crash after the fork now waits the caller's window.** A
  daemon that dies while booting (probe: `os._exit(3)` in the grandchild) is reported after
  30.32 s at head. It took 15.33 s at 5e7f958 and about 45 s at 93d5a0b. The launcher's
  `spawn_daemon` polls ping and never notices that its grandchild has died. That behaviour
  predates this diff. The r6 fix accepted this trade-off, and all four callers run off the
  event loop or in background threads. I am not filing it.
