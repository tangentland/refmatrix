## BULLSHIT {#bullshit}

- **B1. The shutdown worker stage is unbounded for the hub-shared client; self-audit Q4's "≤ 8 s
  per worker, ≤ 16 s for two, 43 s worst" is false.** `daemon.py:1176` (`_close_workers`, the
  `else` branch `w.close(timeout=2.0)`) and `modelsrv.py:170-171`: `SharedWorkerClient.close`
  ignores its timeout and takes `self._lock`, which `call()` holds through send/recv
  (`modelsrv.py:201`). Hub up (the normal state) means `_embed_worker` / `_rerank_worker` are
  `SharedWorkerClient`s with no `retire()`. An op in flight at stop on a slow, cold or mute hub
  blocks `close()` until `SHARED_OP_TIMEOUT_S` = 45 s per role, 90 s for both: past the 45 s grace
  and launchd's ExitTimeOut, so SIGKILL, the ART-corruption path `_StopDeadline` exists to
  prevent. A fleet restart is the realistic overlap. **Measured** (`bsdt03-r1-shared-close-probe.log`):
  mute hub socket, one embed call in flight with a 6 s timeout, the real `Daemon._close_workers`:
  "_close_workers took 5.50s (stage claimed <= 2.0 s lock wait + kill); in-flight call:
  TimeoutError after 6.0 s". The fix covers one of the two classes `new_model_client` builds
  (`recall.py:287-320`); Q3's reason for leaving the shared clients ("hold no warm lock in the
  daemon") is wrong: the lock is the client's own `_lock`. Matches the partial-bound and
  sibling-render-path PATTERN files. **Remedy:** `SharedWorkerClient.retire(timeout,
  abandon_in_flight=False)`: `self._lock.acquire(timeout=)`; on failure `sock.shutdown(SHUT_RDWR)`
  (the holder's recv sees EOF at once), mark retired so `call()`'s reconnect refuses, close.
  `_close_workers` passes abandon to both classes and drops the `close()` fallback. RED: the probe
  as a case in `tests/test_daemon_shutdown.py`, the mute hub a registered fake boundary, asserting
  the stage returns while the call is still held, no clock. Correct Q3/Q4. Self-audit sharpening:
  Q4 "for a stage that iterates over a polymorphic resource, bound each class by name", or a lint
  that every model-client class reachable from `_close_workers` implements `retire(abandon_in_flight=)`. {#b1}

## SKETCHY {#sketchy}

- **S1. The abandon branch's kill and retired flag are unpinned (M3, M4 survive).**
  `subproc.py:392` (`p.kill()`), `:383` (`self._retired = True`). The bug-269 node asserts only that
  the daemon exits before `released`; without the kill, retire waits 2 s and the preload's
  `os.getppid() != _parent: os._exit(0)` cleans up; without the flag, the holder's `call()` hits EOF
  and `_ensure` respawns a model into the stopping daemon. Untested: the kill frees ~610 MB at once;
  the flag prevents a respawn. **Remedy:** drop the preload's ppid self-exit (or only after
  `HOLD_S`); assert the `held` pid is dead before `released` (kills M3) and this launch's `rmxd.log`
  has exactly one `worker[embed] spawned` plus the `not respawning` line (kills M4). {#s1}
- **S2. The two stop guards are reached, not pinned (M5, M6 survive); Q6 cites the bug-269 node as
  their test.** `daemon.py:1250` (warm-up: no reranker load once stopping), `:938` (`_embedder`
  fallback refuses the in-process load). The warm thread is `daemon=True` and `serve_forever` ends in
  `os._exit(0)`, so the ordering assertion cannot see a spawned reranker or a ~610 MB in-process load.
  **Remedy:** the same node asserts `rmxd.log` has "reranker warmup skipped: the daemon is stopping",
  no `worker[rerank] spawned`, no in-process `embedder loaded model=`. Correct Q6: reached is not tested. {#s2}
- **S3. The census's third form (L159 readiness) is fooled 5 of 5; its docstring says "only as a
  DIRECT call".** `tests/budget_outcome_census.py:199-210` matches any `*.wait_models_warm(...)` under
  `ast.walk(fn)` above the first `run_cmd`/`run_hook`. Fooled by: an uncalled lambda; a nested def
  called after `run_cmd`; `try: d.wait_models_warm(timeout=0.01) except Exception: pass`; a different
  receiver; `if False:`. The tree passes legitimately today. **Remedy:** count only a top-level
  `ast.Expr` call (or top-level `with` body), receiver Name equal to the run call's; must-fail cases
  (1)-(5) in `tests/test_budget_outcome_census.py`. {#s3}
