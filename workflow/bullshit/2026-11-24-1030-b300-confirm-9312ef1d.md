## b-1: closed {#b-1}

**The fold.** When `credit_blocks` is true, `_merged` is built from `rows` and from
`_block_rows` without any condition. `_block_len = self._memory_block_doclen(mlid)` is then set
whenever `_scored` is non-empty (`store.py:5326-5347`). Every tf on the credited path is a raw tf,
and every dl on that path is the memory's own postings plus all of its blocks'. {#fold}

**The fixture.** Five memories:

| memory | tags | body | matches through |
|---|---|---|---|
| `longone` | `quokka, wombat` | about 800 words of filler | tags only |
| `shortone` | `quokka, wombat, platypus, echidna` | `Tiny.` | tags only |
| `third` | none | `kettle` | its body |
| `bodyhit` | none | one `wombat` in a short body | a block |
| `bodylong` | none | one `wombat` in a long body | a block |

`quokka` is the tag-only case. `wombat` is the mixed case: two memories match it through their own
postings and two through a block. {#fixture}

{#inproc}

- **The order is stable at the fold.** Across `quokka` and `quokka kettle`, `shortone` stays
  above `longone`. At r1 the two swapped, so b-1 reproduces at r1 and is gone at the fold.
  {#order}
- **Each memory's score keeps a fixed ratio.** Adding `kettle`, a word none of these memories
  contains, multiplies every one of their scores by exactly 0.125. That is the coverage factor
  (1/2)^3, so the quokka or wombat part of each score does not move. At r1 the quokka ratios were
  0.0195 for longone and 0.116 for shortone. {#ratio}
- **Hand BM25 matches the code.** I computed BM25 by hand from SQL with N = 15 and avgdl = 68.6
  from the partition, the partition's df, tf = own + blocks, and dl = own + all blocks. It equals
  `content_rank` to 6 decimals in all 6 single-term cases, both the tag-only ones and the mixed
  ones. Examples:
  - `quokka|longone`: dl 492, 0.491421
  - `quokka|shortone`: dl 15, 2.862906
  - `wombat|bodyhit`: dl 16, 1.936788
  - `wombat|bodylong`: dl 493, 0.335233
  
  At r1 the two tag-only quokka cases were off (3.150 against a hand value of 0.491). The mixed
  wombat cases matched at r1 too, which is why a body-word test never caught b-1. {#hand}

## m-1: the staleness contract is now stated {#m-1}

The `_memory_block_doclen` docstring (`store.py:4998-5004`) now names "the same one-flush staleness
contract as the other BM25 caches". It says a block written since the last write boundary, up to
one flush of about 30 s, is missing from a memory's length until the next one. It records this as
accepted (bsd-b300 r1 m-1, L209). This is enough for an accepted MEH. {#m-1-body}
