**Why it's bullshit:**

- No requirement, AC or consumer-table row puts `memory_add`, the Stop-hook promote, the

  2. `rmx focus summarize --promote --timeout 5` exits 0 in under 5 s and prints "queued".
  3. After release, the digest row and its `part-of` edge exist exactly once.
  4. A kill between enqueue and drain is applied at the next boot.
  5. Mutation: the synchronous call (HEAD) FAILS step 2.
