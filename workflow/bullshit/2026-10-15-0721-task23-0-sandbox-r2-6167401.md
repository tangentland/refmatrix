## Findings {#findings}

### BULLSHIT: the kill deny set leaves out the live hub; the "7777 listener" leg of the S6 fix was dropped {#b1-hub-pid}

**File:** `tests/sandbox_site/rmx_sandbox.py:253-263` (`live_pids`) and `:278`
(`build_deny`, `pids`).

**What:** the live pids are read only from pid files: each root's `rmxd.pid` and each protected
home's `.refmatrix/hub.pid`.

**Why it's bullshit:** on this host the live hub (pid 4191, launchd-supervised, listening on
:7777 since 01:58:42) has no `~/.refmatrix/hub.pid`, so it is not in the deny set. The r1 fix
named three sources: "the deny roots' `rmxd.pid`, the hub pid, **and the 7777 listener**". The
third was dropped, and the summary does not say so. It was the only one that covers the process
r1's S6 was about: "`stop_hub` runs `_pid_on_port(7777)` via `lsof`, then `os.kill`. That is the
live hub, pid 4191 today."

**Evidence:** `bsd-23.0-r2-hubpid.log`, from a scratch test under the gate (deleted afterwards):
`DENY_PIDS [3779, 3834, 3892, 3993, 4002, 4019, 4038, 4050]`,
`GATE_PIDS [... 4050, 4191]`, `MISSING [4191]`, and `PURE_RULE 4191 None`. So
`os.kill(4191, SIGTERM)` from a sandboxed test is not refused. The decoy gate's own probe kept
it safe this round; the sandbox did not. No test reaches it today, because every `stop_hub`
caller mocks `_pid_on_port`.

**Fix:** about 6 lines and 1 test.
1. In `build_deny`, before the hook is installed, add the pids that `lsof -nP -t -iTCP:<p>
   -sTCP:LISTEN` reports for every port in `ports` (7777, plus `RMX_SANDBOX_HUB_PORT`). Use
   absolute `/usr/sbin/lsof`. Name a failure on stderr; do not swallow it.
2. Pin it in `test_build_deny_captures_...` or a sibling: a listener the test owns on a
   deny-extra port appears in `deny["pids"]`.

**Pattern match:** YES. The remedy implemented two of the three prescribed legs and was tested
only against decoy data, never against the host's actual state.
(Related: [[project_bsd_post_deploy_audit_0916]]. Each prescription was narrower than the defect.)

## Observation (not a 23.0 finding) {#obs-hub-pid-file}

The live hub runs without its pid file: `~/.refmatrix/hub.pid` is absent, and `hub.py:932`
writes it at start. As a result, `hub_pid()` returns None on the live host, and `stop_hub`'s
SIGTERM leg cannot find the hub by pid. I have not measured the cause. The hub log shows
`stopping` at 01:58:38 and `starting pid=4191` at 01:58:42. One candidate is the outgoing hub's
unconditional `hub_pid_path().unlink()` (`hub.py:1011`) racing the new hub's write. This is
worth a `bug_registry.md` row.

Correction to r1: it called the shutil exemption "the bug-127 exemption". In the registry, that
exemption is **bug-126**. bug-127 is the brief-real-daemon lock.
