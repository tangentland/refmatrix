### SKETCHY s-1: case (b) ignores room, so a hand-shortened hook is silently reverted {#s-1}

**File:** src/refmatrix/memory_index.py:429 (`_index_hook_wins`) and :408 (`room` reduced to a bool)
**What:** (b) treats any `prefix…` whose prefix starts the sidecar value as "the rendered cut", even on a line that had room for the whole value.
**Evidence:** probe `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-tholley-claude-tools-refmatrix/7aea273d-e348-4816-b218-bb57c4e95046/scratchpad/edge_b.py`. After the next write, the index and the sidecar both show the old full hook again, and the report says nothing. HEAD 61b3bde would have taken the edit, so this is also an unreported marker-off change from HEAD. The spec (#fu-hooks) says "the rendered cut", and a renderer only cuts when `len(cur) > room`.
**Fix:** have `_index_hook_records` return the numeric room (`MAX_LINE_CHARS - len(link_part) - 3`). Then make (b) and (c) require `len(cur) > room`, meaning the line could not carry `cur`. Add the probe shape as a test, marker-off and organised.
