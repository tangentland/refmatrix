## m-b: a VerbError from the fall-through's pack step is one Error: line {#m-b}

**Closed.**

**Was the relocation right? Yes.** r9's body put m-b at `cli.py:12767-12817`. That is
`_memory_recall_impl`, the `rmx memory recall` busy/absent replica fall-through used by
`--session-start`, `--recent` and `--subject`. r9's `rel: related-to -> #r11` edge was wrong:
R11 is recall-state, not this site. The fix and the test sit at the right site.

**The fix** (`83e5aa5`) wraps only the `verbs.pack_recall` call in
`except _verbs.VerbError as pe: raise click.ClickException(str(pe)) from pe`.

**The test** `test_mb_fall_through_verb_error_is_one_error_line` runs the GENERATED SessionStart
argv on a replica store with no daemon. "reading the replica" on stderr proves the fall-through
served. The child process runs the real `cli_entry` with `verbs.pack_recall` replaced by a
function that raises; this is registered in `workflow/test_mock_registry.md`. The test asserts:
exit 1, no `Traceback`, exactly one `Error:` line naming the cause, and 0 B on stdout.

| Mutation | Result |
|---|---|
| mb-narrow: `except _verbs.VerbError` → `except _verbs.VerbArgsError` | **KILLED**: `m-b: a traceback, not one Error: line` |
| mb-reraise: `raise click.ClickException(...)` → bare `raise` | **KILLED**: the same |

**Is there any other VerbError escape in that handler? None.** I read every call between
`except (_verbs.VerbBusyError, _verbs.VerbAbsentError) as e:` (`cli.py:12767`) and the sibling
`except _verbs.VerbError` (`:12824`) at `574c64e`:

  returns.
- `_read_store()`: converts busy to `ClickException` and returns `_store()` when the daemon is
  absent (`cli.py:713-749`).
- `_parse_duration`: `click.BadParameter`.
- `rs.subject_leaves`, `rs.recent_memories`: Store reads. They raise no typed verb error.
- `_verbs.recall_rows_filter`, `_verbs.recent_rows`: pure, no `raise`.
- `_global_rows`: catches `VerbError` around `_global_recall_rows` and its `_left(30.0)` deadline.
  It raises `ClickException` on `scope=global` and warns otherwise. Green's claim is verified.
- `_merge_scope`: pure.
- `_attach_context` (non-pack branch): `verbs.attach_context` catches `require_daemon`'s
  `VerbError` and every per-row failure into `warnings`. `_resolve_partition` →
  `discovery.resolve_partition` makes no daemon call.
