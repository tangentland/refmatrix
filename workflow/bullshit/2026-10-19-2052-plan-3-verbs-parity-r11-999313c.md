## Findings {#findings}

### SKETCHY: the subject-filing leg of the SAME command still says "the daemon may still complete it" for a refused socket — the guard's wrapper set is one level deep {#s-1}

**File:** `src/refmatrix/cli.py:1414-1419` (`_file_under_active_subject`:
`except (TimeoutError, _socket.timeout, OSError)` → `"promoted id=… but subject filing under …
not confirmed within {timeout}s ({e}); the daemon may still complete it"`), which dials through
`_subject_upsert` / `_subject_link` (`:1649`, `:1665`) → `_memory_daemon_call` (`:11184`).
`tests/transport_guard.py:111-117`: the comment says "fixed point over functions that dial"; the
code builds ONE level.

**What:** `focus summarize --promote` makes three daemon writes. The `memory_add` leg (`:1341`) was
routed through `classify_transport_error(write=True)`, and the reason given was that "only a
TIMEOUT means the request may be in the daemon's hands". The two subject-filing writes, 75 lines
further down in the same command, still word every `OSError` themselves. Probe
(`probe_r11_subject.py`, a real refused socket, `daemon_up=True` as the caller passes it):

> `promoted id=1 but subject filing under 'subj-x' not confirmed within 3s ([Errno 61] Connection refused); the daemon may still complete it`

That is the exact claim `write=True` was built to stop making for a refused socket. The guard does
not see it because the dial is two wrappers deep (`_subject_upsert` → `_memory_daemon_call` →
`daemon_mod.call`). Of the 5 new shapes, the guard misses this one (two-level wrapper), plus a
dict-of-messages constant, a `getattr(daemon_mod, "call")` dial and the synonym "no answer". Only
the two-level wrapper occurs in live src; the "no answer" hit is in `reranker.py`, the model
worker, which is out of scope.

**Why SKETCHY, not escalated:** it is reachable only in the race after the status probe said up,
and nothing writes on the wrong side. But this is the third round running in which "one
classifier" was falsified by a sibling of the quoted line, and here the sibling is in the same
command.

**Fix (about 3 lines plus the guard):**

- Route the handler through `classify_transport_error(e, op="subject_upsert/link", root=…,
  timeout=…, write=True)`, keeping "promoted id=… but subject filing …" as the prefix.
- Make `Corpus.wrappers` the fixed point its own comment describes: iterate until no new function
  dials through a known wrapper.
- Plant the two-level shape.

**Pattern match:** YES. These are [[bsd-impressions#imp-quoted-line-sibling]] and
[[bsd-impressions#imp-ast-guard-one-spelling]].
