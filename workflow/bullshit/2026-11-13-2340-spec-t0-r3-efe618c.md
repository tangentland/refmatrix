## Findings {#findings}

### MEH: a trial's hang is charged to the row even when the target test did not hang {#m-1}

**File:** `#t0-3-cap` `:383-387` ("Hitting the cap counts as a **failure** … A hang in the base
trials counts toward the reproducer"), `#t0-3-band-found` `:388` ("≥ 3 failures in 10 trials at
base, **each with the row's E line**"), `#t0-3-band-fixed` ("Any failure in the 45, a hang
included, means that fix attempt did not hold").

The cap applies to the whole trial: the target test plus 3 to 8 background processes running the
heaviest files. A background process that hangs (the bug-088 class, multiplied by this harness)
causes two problems:
- **Base trials.** It counts toward the reproducer, although a hang carries no E line. That
  contradicts `:388`, and it inflates the base rate with failures that are not the row's.
- **Fix attempts.** It fails a fix attempt. Two unrelated background hangs can use up both
  attempts, revert a sound fix, and mark the row `diagnosed-loud`.

The `ps`/`lsof` record makes this visible afterwards, but the band rules act on it before anyone
reads it.

**Fix (one sentence in `#t0-3-cap`):** a trial's outcome is the **target test's**. A cap hit
counts as the row's failure (`hang`) only if the target test had not finished, as the `ps` record
shows. If the target finished and a background process hung, the trial is void: it is recorded,
it is not counted, and it is re-run, at most 3 voids per level.
