### BULLSHIT b-1-r2: after a `--save`, the next compiling brief counts its own saved rows as unembedded memories {#b-1-r2}

**File:** `src/refmatrix/brief.py` (`_plan_unembedded`, and its use in `compile_briefs`).

**What:** `_plan_unembedded` does arithmetic on the PLAN's corpus: `stats.unembedded`, capped by `candidates` minus the rows the plan placed. The plan's corpus is `consolidate.DEFAULT_EXCLUDE_MTYPES` (`session`, `digest`, `subject`). The brief's corpus (`_memory_ids`) also excludes its own output, `brief`/`brief/*` (`brief.EXCLUDE_MTYPES`). Rows saved by `brief --save` are in the plan's corpus but not the brief's. Until they are embedded they are "unembedded" in the plan, so they are counted as skipped memories of a corpus they are not in.

**Evidence:** probe C4 (`test_c4_save_then_compile`, real spawned daemon, mini lane, `workflow/review-output/bsd-t05-c-tip.log:9-10,31-34`):

```
C4 saved: 15
C4 second: memories=21 skipped=18 skipped_by={'unembedded': 18} by_class={'corroborated': 1, 'singleton': 14}
E   assert 18 == 3
```

18 = the 3 real unembedded memories + the 15 saved brief rows. The CLI's line then says "18 row(s) skipped (unembedded=18) — counted, not dropped" over 21 memories. 15 of those 18 are not memories of the corpus the report describes. This happens on the plan's documented workflow (save, then brief again), and the overcount grows with every brief saved.

**Why it's bullshit:**
- The lead asked for no double count and no undercount. This is an overcount: the skip total counts rows outside `stats.memories`. That makes the "counted, not dropped" line false in the other direction.
- It also breaks 26.6 #rm-one-corpus ("one report reads one corpus"), because the count is taken from the plan's corpus instead of the brief's.
- The correct count is cheaper than the one shipped. `compile_briefs` already holds `rows`, the brief's own corpus. The rows the plan placed nowhere are a set difference, and that needs no `stats` arithmetic.

**Fix I'd accept:**
1. In `compile_briefs`, take `placed = {member names of every cluster} ∪ set(plan["unclustered"])`, then `n = sum(1 for r in rows.values() if r["name"] not in placed)`, and delete `_plan_unembedded`'s arithmetic on stats. This is exact on the early-return, concept-only and `max_subjects` paths (C2/C3/C5 all reduce to it) and for a caller-built plan. Optionally name the reason `unembedded` when the plan's `stats.embedded < candidates`, and `not_in_plan` otherwise (a stale caller plan).
2. RED first: add "save, force a snapshot, brief again" to the real-daemon entry-point test (C4 is the shape) and see `18 == 3` fail.
3. Also acceptable, and the one-corpus root fix: the op's `compile_memories(s, scope=…)` passes `exclude_mtypes=brief.EXCLUDE_MTYPES`, so the brief's own output never enters the plan. This alone does not cover a caller-built `plan=` (the dry-run `memory_compile` plan includes brief rows), so (1) is still needed.

**Pattern match:** YES. Same class as r1 #b-1 and r4 #s-2-r4: a skip total that does not reconcile with the corpus the report states.
