# Lead rulings ledger {#root}

Every lead ruling is an `L<n>` row here ([[orchestration#ledgers]]). Rulings are **evidence**:
the state they change (order, scope, status) is written into [[plan-of-plans]] in the same
commit ([[orchestration#pop-truth]]); a row here never holds state the POP lacks. Rows L1-L124
stay in their original home, [[plan-review-2026-11-13#lead]] (archived 2026-11-15, the user:
"archive any other doc that acts as an alternate source of truth"); their ids and links are
unchanged.
